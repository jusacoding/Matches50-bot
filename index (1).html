Strategy and trading

A digit qualifies in a market when it is the single most frequent digit in the last 25, 50 and 100 ticks and its average share is at least the minimum. The market with the highest share is suggested. Each digit still has a fixed 10% chance per tick, so this strategy has no real edge.
